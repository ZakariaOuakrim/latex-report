\chapter*{Introduction}

The modern world is characterized by an exponential explosion of data,
driven by factors such as increasing digitalization, the Internet of
Things (IoT), and intelligent systems. This abundance of information
has transformed the way organizations operate, creating unprecedented
opportunities while also posing significant challenges in the
management and analysis of large scale data. In this context, Big Data
has become an essential pillar for organizations aiming to fully
leverage their data and make strategic decisions based on meaningful
insights.

This project is part of an academic initiative focused on building a
real time Big Data ecosystem for IoT sensor data. The main objective
is to design and implement an environment capable of efficiently
managing the massive amount of data generated by connected devices and
sensor networks. Such an ecosystem provides a foundation for projects
in domains such as smart cities, industrial monitoring, and
transportation optimization.

To achieve this goal, the project adopts a comprehensive approach that
integrates key concepts of Big Data, including data ingestion,
distributed storage, real-time stream processing, advanced analytics,
and data visualization. The aim is not only to handle data at scale,
but also to extract actionable insights that can drive innovation and
improve decision-making.

This introduction lays the groundwork for exploring the different
stages of the project, from the analysis of requirements to the
implementation of the Big Data ecosystem, highlighting the integration
of various components, the results obtained, and the future
perspectives. This report therefore presents a strategic and
technological approach to transforming the way IoT data is managed and
utilized.
